## Context
Coperniq needed to expand beyond solar contractors and turn traffic into demos that matched its broader platform vision.

## What changed
- Positioning, homepage, and industry pages now speak to HVAC, electrical, and other complex trades.
- Demo experience rebuilt to keep people moving past the first step and to capture structured attribution.
- Offline conversion loop feeds SQL/opportunity stages back into Meta for higher-quality acquisition.

## Outcome
Non-solar demo volume and completion rates improved while sales gained cleaner channel-level signal for pipeline-quality decisions.
