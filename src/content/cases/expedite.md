## Context
Expedite Studio needed to convert heightened interest around a launch window. Visitors landed, skimmed, and bounced because the value story and proof points were buried.

## What changed
- Rewrote and reorganized landing pages around distinct user intents and objections.
- Visualized credibility with demo GIFs, social proof, and risk-reversal elements.
- Ran rapid A/B tests to tune messaging and layout before the launch deadline.

## Outcome
Conversion and traffic quality improved ahead of launch, giving the team confidence in the funnel and messaging architecture.
